# How to Export Minecraft PE Worlds on Android | Fix Data Folder Hidden (ZArchiver & Shizuku)

Export Minecraft PE World to Android (No PC Needed) Welcome to the video! Do you want to save your amazing Minecraft world, back it up, or send it to a friend? Because of new Android security rules, your phone hides the Minecraft folder. But don't worry! Today, I will teach you a super easy trick to find and copy your worlds using two free apps. Follow these simple steps like a secret mission! ️‍ ️

Step-by-Step Tutorial (Very Easy):

Step 1: Unlock the Secret "Developer" Menu Open your phone's Settings. Go to About Phone (or Software Information). Find Build Number and tap on it 7 times fast! Now, go back to Settings and open the new Developer Options menu. Scroll down and turn ON Wireless Debugging (Note: Your phone must be connected to Wi-Fi for this to work!).

Step 2: Start the Shizuku Magic Open the Shizuku app. Tap on Pairing. Tap on the Developer Options button on the screen. Tap directly on the text that says "Wireless debugging". Tap on "Pair device with pairing code". You will see a big 6-digit number on your screen. Swipe down your notification bar from the top. You will see a Shizuku notification. Type that 6-digit number into the notification and hit send! Go back to the Shizuku app and tap Start. Now Shizuku is running in the background!
