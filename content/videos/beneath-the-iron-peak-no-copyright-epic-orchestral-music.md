## Use Karne Se Pehle

1. Video mein track sun kar confirm karein ki mood aur duration aapke edit ke liye suitable hai.
2. Apne project mein use karne se pehle original uploader/creator ki current licence, attribution rules aur monetisation terms check karein.
3. Licence ya written permission ko save rakhein, agar available ho. “No copyright” title akela legal permission ka proof nahi hota; YouTube par claim na aane ki guarantee bhi nahi deta.
4. Agar visuals reuse karne hain, toh music permission ko video footage permission se alag samjhein.
