# SugerCane Farm For MCPE || For ALL VERSION || Tutorial

Minecraft PE mein **Sugar Cane Farm** banana seekhein. Sugar cane ko grow karne ke liye suitable block par water ke bilkul adjacent plant karna hota hai; mature cane ko harvest karke paper aur sugar crafting mein use kiya ja sakta hai. Video ka title tutorial/farm build ko indicate karta hai, lekin is record mein exact block-by-block layout ya required-material list included nahi hai, isliye precise build details ke liye video ko follow karein.

## Farm Banate Waqt Basic Process

1. Water ke paas level ground choose karein aur cane ke liye row/space plan karein.
2. Suitable block (jaise dirt, grass ya sand) ko directly water ke side mein place/prepare karein.
3. Sugar cane plant karein aur grow hone ke liye time dein. Plant ko grow karne ke liye light source required nahi hota, lekin chunk/simulation distance ke bahar growth pause ho sakti hai.
4. Agar video mein automatic/semi-automatic harvesting design dikhaya gaya hai, toh pistons/redstone/collection area ka placement video ke exact design se match karein.
5. Harvest ke baad sugar cane collect karein aur replant/base layer ko preserve karein.
6. Farm ko kuch growth cycles tak test karein taaki drops collection aur design work karte hain ya nahi verify ho.

## Useful Information

Sugar cane paper aur sugar dono craft karne mein kaam aata hai. Farm ki speed aur automatic collection Minecraft edition, design aur loaded simulation area par depend karte hain.

**Version note:** “All Version” title ko guarantee na samjhein; redstone design updates ke baad different behave kar sakta hai.
