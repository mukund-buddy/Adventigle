# Bypass the "Impossible" Red Border in Minecraft PE

Minecraft PE mein on-screen controls ko move karte waqt edge par red boundary aa rahi hai aur button ko expected jagah nahi rakh pa rahe? Is video mein HUD/control editor ke behaviour ko exploit karne wali ek placement trick dikhayi gayi hai. Process screen-animation/anti-reset setting, boundary ke paas button position karne aur reset icon se placement ko adjust karne par based hai. Yeh regular supported feature nahi, isliye update ke baad kaam karna band kar sakta hai.

## Steps Covered

1. Minecraft ki control/customisation screen open karein.
2. Video mein dikhayi gayi **Screen Animation / Anti-Reset** setting ko check karein; menu label aapke version mein different ho sakta hai.
3. Button ko red border ke paas place karke “size-to-border” adjustment ka method follow karein.
4. **Reset icon** ka use usi tarah karein jaisa video mein dikhaya gaya hai, phir button ki final position verify karein.
5. Editor se bahar aakar gameplay mein controls test karein. Agar layout reset ho jaaye ya touch button inaccessible ho, normal layout restore karein.

## Important Notes

- Yeh trick specific game/UI behaviour par depend karti hai; har version ya device par same result ki guarantee nahi.
- Experiment se pehle current control layout ka screenshot rakhna useful hai.
- Controls ko aisi jagah na rakhein jahan essential movement/action buttons touch karna mushkil ho.

Is video ka focus settings-based HUD workaround hai, Resource Pack download nahi.
