## Reuse Karne Ka Workflow

1. Track ko video mein sun kar apne montage ki pacing ke saath compare karein.
2. Agar edit mein voice commentary hai, music volume lower rakhein aur important gameplay sounds ko audible rakhein.
3. Upload se pehle original track page/uploader se current licence aur attribution requirements verify karein; video title mein “Copyright Free” likha hona apne aap permission establish nahi karta.
4. Music use karne ki permission aur kisi accompanying footage ki permission ko separate check karein.
