# Transform Your Totem: Minecraft Custom Skin Tutorial

Is tutorial ka focus Minecraft PE mein **Totem of Undying** ki default texture ko apne custom design se replace karna hai. Totem ka texture game mein ek specific item appearance ko control karta hai; texture pack banane par original artwork ko apni custom image se replace karke pack ke through load kiya ja sakta hai. Video title custom-skin style totem design ke workflow ko point karta hai.

## Typical Workflow

1. Pehle apne Minecraft version ke liye appropriate resource-pack template/structure ready karein.
2. Totem texture asset ko identify karein aur backup copy preserve karein.
3. Image editor mein custom design ko original texture ki required dimensions/transparency ke mutabiq prepare karein. Exact filename, resolution aur folder path pack template/version par depend karte hain.
4. Edited texture ko original asset ke matching path par rakhein aur required `manifest.json`/pack metadata verify karein.
5. Pack ko Minecraft mein import karein aur **Global Resources** ya relevant world ke Resource Packs section mein activate karein.
6. World load karke Totem of Undying ka appearance test karein. Texture apply na ho toh filename, folder nesting, image format, pack priority aur manifest check karein.

## Important Note

Is source record mein template, editor ya downloadable project ka direct link list nahi hai, isliye koi specific tool/download invent nahi kiya gaya. Video ke visuals ko follow karein aur original files ko overwrite karne se pehle backup rakhein.
